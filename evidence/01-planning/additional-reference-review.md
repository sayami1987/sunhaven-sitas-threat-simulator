# Additional source review during Phase 6

The user added three example reports and NIST, SANS and ASD links while the
implementation was in progress. The official sources were checked and their
relevance is documented in `docs/methodology/reference-alignment.md`.

The three supplied PDF examples were reviewed for structure, evidence presentation
and limitations. No visible course identifier establishes them as governing unit
instructions. One explicitly describes itself as an altered previous-capstone
example and warns that assessment requirements can differ. The supplied
COIT13236 specifications and rubric remain the assessment sources.

Useful reporting ideas include a clear purpose and context, numbered findings,
asset/threat/control links, captioned diagrams, evidence tables and measurable
technical goals. SITAS retains its own original text, actual synthetic findings
and documented risk convention. The examples' sensitive details, contact lists,
figures and assessment claims were not imported into the public repository.

The example risk report uses a different asset-sensitivity calculation. It is
not evidence for replacing SITAS's likelihood-times-impact model. Inconsistent
jurisdictional references, incomplete example sections and unverified technical
recommendations were identified in the private review notes and not adopted.
